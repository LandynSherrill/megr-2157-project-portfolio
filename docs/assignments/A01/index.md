a} The primary function of the adjustable wrench is to apply torque to an object after fitting the jaws onto it.

b} Equation: T = (W*k<sup>2</sup>*a)/g (T = Torque, W = weight, k = radius, a = angular acceleration, g = gravity) . One assumption that makes this model a valid descriptor of this product is the assumption of a fixed attachment point, to ensure consistent values.

c} For the base, it's geometry allows the wrench to be more easily gripped, increasing the potential torque by extending the distance from the focus that a force can be applied, providing enough structure to allow harsher use without risking damage to the wrench, as well as providing the first jaw surface that is in contact with the chosen object. For the moving jaw, It is shaped to slot into the groove of the base, allowing it room to move easily when being adjusted, and has the second jaw surface that is in contact with the chosen object. The third component, the knurl, is shaped and positioned to catch the moving jaw as a worm gear, allowing for minute adjustments by catching the teeth of the moving jaw while simultaneously minimizing the profile and material usage.

d} With the design of the wrench being non-proprietary, it does not have an explicit patent.\
i. For alternative solutions, we have a standard wrench, which is functionally the same without being adjustable, as well as a ratcheting wrench, often having a fixed jaw on one side and a ratcheting socket on the other.\
ii. If we look at the adjustable wrench, the slot on the base that the moving jaw fits into passes through the entire base, with open ends. This seemingly allows the moving jaw to extend to the maximum, retract to the minimum, and still have enough teeth for the knurl to catch while also not wasting more material and space to fully enclose the jaw's rail.
