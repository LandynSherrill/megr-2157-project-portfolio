#About Me

My name is Landyn Sherrill.\
I am a student seeking to become a mechanical engineer. I have been interested in the field for a while, and took the opportunity to delve into it when one was presented. The primary appeal of mechanical engineering for me was the broad spectrum of applications, as well as the seemingly greater emphasis on the whole product, rather than designated sections of it. While many fields tend to box themselves into their niche, mechanical engineering stands as something that can operate as the connection between the niche groups. There is a lot of potential in that, and I would like to see it recognized even greater. I intend on becoming a more proficient designer and creator, and I will be working towards that in courses such as this one.

"What does it mean to defend an engineering decision : and do you currently know how to do it?"\
I would say that, to defend an engineering decision, is to secure the position as not only valid, but sufficient at least and ideal at best. To be honest, I do not currently know how to go about that, but I can speculate that it would involve laying out all of the pertinent information, and using that to point to the chosen decision being the best for the job.
